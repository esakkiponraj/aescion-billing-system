# Product Scope & Business Profiles

## 1. Product Vision
AESCION is a next-generation commerce operations operating system. It merges rapid point-of-sale execution with deep back-office control, inventory precision, and proactive business pulse analytics.

## 2. Business Profiles (Supported Verticals)
The platform adapts dynamically based on the selected Business Profile during onboarding:

| Profile | Target Segment | Key Modules Enabled |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Retail Shop** | Clothing, Electronics, General Trade | POS, Inventory, Customers, Purchases, Barcode Engine |
| **Supermarket** | Multi-lane grocery, convenience chains | Multi-Register POS, Batch & Expiry, Price Lists, Fast Search |
| **Wholesale / Distribution** | B2B Traders, Distributors | Quotations, Sales Orders, Credit Limits, Tiered Pricing, Invoicing |
| **Restaurant / Cafe** | QSR, Dine-in, Cafes | Dine-in Floor Plans, Tables, KOT, Kitchen Stations, Modifiers, Recipes |
| **Service Business** | Repair shops, Salons, Agencies | Service Items, Labor Invoicing, Customer Profiles, Appointments |
| **Pharmacy** | Medical stores, Chemists | Drug Schedules, Batch/Expiry Tracking, Prescription Refs |

## 3. Core Persona Workflows
* **Cashier**: 1-click product addition, barcode scan, hold/resume bills, multi-tender payment (Cash, UPI, Card, Credit), fast print.
* **Store Manager**: Monitor active registers, review on-duty staff, approve cash variances, grant price overrides/refunds.
* **Business Owner**: View "Business Pulse", real-time anomaly alerts (unusual discounts, stockouts, cash discrepancies), high-level profitability.
* **Accountant**: Manage purchase bills, credit notes, debit notes, tax summaries, ledger exports.
* **SaaS Super Admin**: Multi-tenant metrics, feature flags, plan tiers, time-limited Support Mode impersonation.
